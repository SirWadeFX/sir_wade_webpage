Frequently asked questions

What skill level is this for?

Intermediate to advanced. If you’re earlier in your journey, Animation Basics + Fundamentals is the better place to start.

What software is used?

Demos are mostly in Maya, but the concepts are software-agnostic and carry over to Blender or Unreal.

Are assignments mandatory?

No. They’re there to practice what you learn, and nobody’s being graded.

How long will I have access?

Forever. It’s a one-time purchase, and the recordings never expire.

Can I get feedback on my shots?

The class is pre-recorded. For personal notes and drawovers on your work, book a Mentorship Dailies session.
