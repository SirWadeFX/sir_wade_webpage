How it works

  1. Schedule a session

    Pick a 30-minute time slot
    on the Mentorship Calendar.
    New slots open every month.

  2. Submit your shot

    Send your shot ahead
    as a SyncSketch link,
    at least an hour before.

  3. Live session

    Get live notes
    and drawovers on your shot.

  4. Receive recording

    Receive your session’s recording
    for your own use.
