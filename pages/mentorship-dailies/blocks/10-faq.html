Frequently asked questions

How long are mentorship sessions? Can I book more than one time slot?

Each session is 30 minutes. Book as many slots as you like, whenever they’re open.

I can’t make it to my time slot. Can I change it after booking?

Can I record my mentorship session?

You don’t need to. You get the recording of every session to keep.

Do I need to be enrolled in a class to get a mentorship session?

No. Dailies are open to everyone: current students of Sir Wade’s classes or another program, graduates, and people who’ve never enrolled anywhere.
