Frequently asked questions

Is this class for beginners or intermediate animators?

It’s built for beginners, but plenty of graduates and working animators take it to re-learn the mechanics or un-learn bad habits. Revisiting the fundamentals always pays off.

How long will I have access?

Forever. It’s a one-time purchase, and the recordings never expire.

What software can I use?

Whatever you like. Demos are mostly in Maya, but Blender and UE5 work just as well.

How much time should I put into assignments?

Aim for at least 2 hours of animating a day, and about 1 to 2 weeks per assignment. Go at your own pace: progress is the win, not perfection.

Can I get feedback on my shots?

The class is pre-recorded. For personal notes and drawovers on your work, book a Mentorship Dailies session.
